# Target definitions and Stage 1 reconciliation
RECOMMENDATION: primary regression target is released Dexcom glucose in mg/dL at exactly t+30 elapsed minutes. It is not blood-glucose ground truth, a native-observation label or a clinical adverse-event outcome. No new target interpolation is introduced.

MEASURED: every participant's one-minute complete-history count matches Stage 1. Five-minute sampling selects a deterministic subset of the same origins, not a new source or measurement cadence. Paired feature sets share all target/origin IDs. event_definition_comparison.csv keeps secondary labels and regression eligibility separate.

Secondary thresholds are strictly >180 and >250 mg/dL, for exploratory research only. A qualifying onset s requires 30 valid cells <=threshold at s-30,...,s-1 and at least 15 valid above-threshold cells s,...,s+14. Gaps interrupt both conditions. Operational separation does not imply statistical independence.

Origin states are mutually exclusive: invalid_current; currently_above; below_insufficient_recovery; eligible_at_risk. At-risk requires 30 consecutive valid non-high cells t-29,...,t. Excluded states have no binary outcome. An at-risk positive has a confirmed qualifying onset in (t,t+30]; the actual decision-information end is earliest such onset+14. An at-risk negative requires no qualifying onset and complete valid future observations t+1,...,t+44. Without a known positive and complete required support, outcome is censored with label missing, not zero. This conservative negative policy can censor more than the minimum logically necessary; it is predeclared and not tuned.

label_information_start=t-29 includes origin recovery. max_label_information_end=t+44 for every candidate records worst-case horizon support. A positive at onset t+30 is confirmed only through t+44. actual_decision_information_end is the earlier confirmation for a known positive, t+44 for a known negative, and missing for excluded/censored cases. Purges must include the full maximum interval if using a common conservative support boundary; never only the 30-minute onset horizon.

Stage 1 any-crossing positives required valid current glucose <=threshold and a fully observed [t,t+30] interval containing any higher value, with no 15-minute duration or 30-minute recovery requirement. Stage 2 sustained at-risk positives change BOTH duration and recovery rules. They are therefore not interchangeable counts. An early sustained positive can remain knowable even if a gap later in the 30-minute future interval makes the Stage 1 any-crossing label unobservable. Overlap/decomposition columns explicitly record this rather than assuming a strict subset.

Episode counts use the whole one-minute release, independent of whether a regression origin has complete prior 60-minute history or lies on the five-minute issue grid. Candidate-origin counts, regression-eligible origin counts and qualifying episodes must be reported separately. None establishes classifier accuracy or probability calibration.
